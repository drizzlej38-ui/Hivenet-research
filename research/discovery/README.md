# Discovery Systems and Open-Ended Development

**Status:** Exploratory research track  
**Maturity:** Conceptual proposals; no implementation or emergent result is claimed.  
**Date:** 2026-11-26

## Purpose

Investigate whether seeded, open-domain knowledge discovery, independent validation, and a developmental computational environment can produce useful findings or capabilities not prescribed in advance.

**Central question:** Can an ecosystem of computational components develop useful organization or capabilities through open-ended interaction, when given conditions for operation and development but no assigned intelligence objective or prescribed developmental destination?

This is a research question, not an assumption that intelligence or superintelligence will emerge.

## Working definitions

- **Seed:** An initial topic, question, observation, dataset, or capability gap. It starts exploration but does not permanently define its limits.
- **Discovery engine:** A process that explores information, generates candidate relationships or hypotheses, preserves provenance, and routes candidates for validation.
- **Candidate discovery:** A potentially useful relationship, explanation, anomaly, method, or capability opportunity not yet validated.
- **Capability gap:** A mismatch between a demonstrated task requirement and currently available capabilities.
- **Developmental ecosystem:** A computational environment in which components, connections, roles, or configurations can change through defined processes and interactions.
- **Computational phenotype:** An observable component, capability, topology, behavior, or configuration produced by developmental processes. This is an analogy, not a claim of biological equivalence.
- **Ecological autonomy:** Internal development and reorganization without an externally prescribed destination.
- **Outer containment:** Technical isolation and resource limits protecting systems outside the experiment.

## Research integrity

Distinguish established findings, interpretations, project-defined concepts, hypotheses, speculation, and negative results. Candidate generation is not validation. Novelty, complexity, persistence, or model agreement are not sufficient evidence of truth, intelligence, or fitness.

## Relationship to other HiveNet work

This track is intended to interoperate with existing research on adaptive neurogenesis/dynamic neural meshes, Mesh Neural Networking, and cooperative intelligence. It does not replace those tracks or assume their proposals are validated. ENDCAP may be evaluated later as a provenance/history layer; integration is not yet implemented.

The discovery engine may surface candidate knowledge and capability gaps. Separate validation, development, evaluation, and authorization processes determine whether anything is built or adopted.

## Initial sequence

1. Define the discovery process and evidence record.
2. Pilot seeded exploration on a bounded, inspectable corpus.
3. Compare direct, associative, contrarian, and anomaly-led exploration.
4. Test independent validation and provenance.
5. Build a minimal ecosystem simulator without an intelligence objective.
6. Compare open-ended, directed, and environment-responsive conditions.
7. Test whether validated findings yield measurable downstream capability improvements.

## Non-goals

This work does not claim that ASI is achievable, provide unrestricted external access to an experimental system, allow discoveries to authorize their own deployment, or treat biological analogies as proof of computational equivalence.
